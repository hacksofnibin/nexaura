Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 27, 2026 · Effective immediately

📋 Introduction

Welcome to NexAura ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and what rights you have in relation to it.

By using NexAura, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of our services.

🍪 Cookie Policy

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your experience. Here is a breakdown of the cookies we use:

  • Essential Cookies: Required for the website to function. Cannot be disabled. (e.g., session cookies)
  • Preference Cookies: Remember your category interests and reading preferences to personalize your news feed.
  • Analytics Cookies: Help us understand how visitors interact with the website (used only with your consent).
  • Authentication Cookies: Keep you signed in to your NexAura account across sessions.

You can control cookie preferences via our cookie consent banner that appears on your first visit. You may also clear cookies at any time through your browser settings.

📦 Information We Collect

  • Account Information: Name and email address if you create an account or sign in via social login.
  • Reading Preferences: Categories and articles you interact with (stored locally via cookies, with your consent).
  • Comments: Any comments you submit on articles are stored in your browser's local storage — they are not sent to our servers.
  • Technical Data: Browser type, device type, pages visited, and time on site (Analytics only with consent).
  • Voluntarily Provided: Any information you provide when contacting us.

🔧 How We Use Your Information

  • To personalize your news feed based on your reading history and interests.
  • To maintain your login session and account preferences.
  • To improve the website's content, performance, and user experience.
  • To display articles and news content relevant to you.
  • To respond to your inquiries and support requests.

🔗 Third-Party Services & Sources

NexAura aggregates news content from third-party publishers. We credit all original sources and link back directly. We do not claim ownership of third-party content. Please review individual publisher privacy policies for their specific data practices:

  • TechCrunch – techcrunch.com
  • WIRED – wired.com
  • MIT Technology Review – technologyreview.com
  • VentureBeat – venturebeat.com
  • The Hacker News – thehackernews.com

Images displayed are sourced from Unsplash (unsplash.com) under their respective licenses.

Social Login (Google, LinkedIn, GitHub)

When you choose to sign in using a social provider, we receive basic profile information (name, email) from that provider. We do not store your social media passwords. The OAuth flow is handled securely by the respective provider. We use this information only to create and maintain your NexAura account.

🛡️ Data Storage & Security

NexAura is a client-side application. Most data (reading preferences, comments) is stored locally in your browser via cookies and localStorage — it does not leave your device. We implement industry-standard security practices to protect any data that does reach our servers.

⚖️ Your Rights (GDPR / Privacy Rights)

  • Right to Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Correction: Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Right to Deletion: Request that we delete your personal data ("right to be forgotten").
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Withdraw cookie consent at any time by clearing your browser cookies or revisiting the cookie settings.
  • Right to Portability: Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Right to Object: Object to processing of your data for certain purposes.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at privacy@nexaura.ai.

👶 Children's Privacy

NexAura is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately.

🔄 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any significant changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of NexAura after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
