About CampusVotex

CampusVotex is a college-focused complaint management platform where students report problems and vote for priorities. Staff members update issue status as the work progresses, making resolution transparent and accountable for everyone.

  • Students can post and vote issues.
  • Teachers / staff can update issue status and close issues.
  • High-voted unresolved issues trigger staff alerts.
  • Similar issues are auto-merged using AI-style text similarity logic.
